Common questions

      
Before you call

    

    

      
How fast can you deliver an emergency AC unit?
Same day. Dispatch gives you an arrival time on the call, and some customers have had units running within the hour.

      
Is any special wiring or a permit required?
No. All units run on standard 110V/15A outlets, and no building permit is required for temporary HOA or condo use.

      
Are the units quiet enough for overnight use?
Yes. Our premium LG and Midea models have quiet modes as low as 42 dB.

      
My condo or HOA needs paperwork before a vendor comes in. Can you handle that?
Yes. Many buildings ask vendors for proof of insurance. We send it directly to your building or HOA manager, usually within an hour, so nothing holds up delivery.
